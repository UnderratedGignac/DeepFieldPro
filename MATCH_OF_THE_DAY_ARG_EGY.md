# 🏟️ Argentina vs Egypt — Our Call

**Prediction: Argentina win 🇦🇷 — predicted, but deliberately NOT bet. Here's why.**

Our agent (DeepFieldPro) rates Argentina at **80.8%** to beat Egypt — the strongest
consensus pick of the quarter-finals across our full signal stack. We submitted it
as our ledger prediction.

But we staked **$0** on it, because the market for this match is broken:

**1. The displayed price is an illusion.**
Argentina "trades" at 8.5¢ — but pull the raw order book and look at what's
actually there:

```
ARGENTINA YES — live Polymarket book
BIDS (buyers):            ASKS (sellers):
  0.05 x 73,594             0.95 x 16,560
  0.04 x 52,301             0.96 x 20,650
  0.03 x 148,027            0.97 x 44,545
```

Buyers at 1–5¢, cheapest seller at **95¢** — a **90-cent-wide spread**. The
"midpoint" is the average of junk on both sides. There is no market here.

**2. We tested it, hard.**
Our agent fired fill-or-kill orders every 10 minutes for 12+ hours, capped at 70¢.
Every single one was rejected — *there is no seller at or below fair value*.
Egypt's side of the market never even got a token. Buying at the real ask (95¢)
would mean risking $44 to win $2.30 on an 81% shot — sharply negative EV.

**3. Above 70¢, the bet is dead money.**
Paying ~80¢ on an 81% shot means risking $44 to win about $11, for an expected
profit of roughly zero. A favorite bought above fair value is how tournament
bankrolls quietly bleed out.

**4. The capital went where the mispricing actually is.**
Same betting window, different match — one with real liquidity and a real gap
between price and probability. That's where our stake landed instead.

---

The rule our agent trades on: **you don't bet the winner, you bet the wrong price.**

Argentina is the best team on the pitch today — and the worst bet on the board.
